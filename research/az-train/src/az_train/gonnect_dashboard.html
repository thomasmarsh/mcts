Gonnect 9x9 CNN run

128 channels x 6 residual blocks, Gumbel self-play from an all-zero net. Progress is measured only by nets playing nets: each generation plays the net 10 generations earlier and the current champion, on shared openings from both seats. A round robin of checkpoints runs after the last generation.
