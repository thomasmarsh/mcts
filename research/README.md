# Technical Notes

This folder contains results that I could not find in the literature and which
may represent original findings, whether positive or negative. These are almost
universally written by Claude and generally fall under the umbrella of
"strategy fusion" -- i.e., the combination of MCTS techniques and extensions
that I have no examples of in the wild and which present questions or seem
in conflict. Many of these fusions are around paralellism (analagous to
virtual loss in parallel tree search) or graph search (where move sequence or
player attribution can become ambiguous).
